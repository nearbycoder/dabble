Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Dabble doesn't collect anything. There's no Dabble server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your to-dos stay on your devices, and on your own sync server, encrypted, if you choose to run one.

What the apps store

Your to-dos, projects, areas, tags and settings are saved as a JSON file inside Dabble's sandboxed app container on each device. Your sync group key and the list of devices you've paired are saved next to it in sync.json. Nothing is stored anywhere else unless you export a backup yourself.

What the apps send

Dabble talks only to your own devices:

  • It announces itself on your local network with Bonjour (_dabble._tcp) so your other devices can find it. The announcement carries the device name you chose, a random device ID and a short fingerprint of your sync group. It never contains your to-dos.
  • It connects directly to devices in your sync group, or to addresses you type in yourself, and exchanges changes over an encrypted channel. Devices without your sync key can't read anything.

The apps don't contact any server run by us or by anyone else. There is no telemetry, crash reporting or update check.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device's operating system. No push notification service is involved.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads are served from GitHub Releases.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data goes with it. To take a device out of your sync group, leave the group from Settings → Sync on that device. Leaving keeps the data that is already on it.

Questions

Dabble is open source, so you can check every claim on this page in the code. Questions are welcome as a GitHub issue, and security reports go through the process in SECURITY.md.
